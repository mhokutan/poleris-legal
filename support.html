Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? In the App: Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your cloud data.
  • Do I need an account? No. Intentions, tasks, notes, rituals and your vision board live on your device. An account is only needed if you want to sync your profile.
  • Where are the natal-chart features? They are optional. Add your birth date (and, if you know it, time and place) in Profile; your daily note and compatibility cards then use your real chart.
  • How do reminders work? Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server. Manage them in Profile → Notifications.
  • How do I manage my subscription? Poleris Premium is a monthly auto-renewing subscription ($4.99/month, 3-day free trial). Manage or cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions; cancellation stops future renewals.
