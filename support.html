Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? In the App: Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your cloud data.
  • My birth time is unknown. Choose "I don't know my exact birth time" during setup; your Sun and Moon signs stay accurate, and you can add the exact time later for a sharper chart.
  • How do I manage my subscription? Subscriptions are managed through your App Store account settings and can be cancelled at any time.
