Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Poleris ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Poleris mobile application (the "App").

Information we collect

  • Content you create. Your intentions, tasks, journal and gratitude notes, daily ritual entries, affirmation preferences, vision board photos and drawings, and compatibility cards are stored on your device.
  • Account information (optional). If you create an account: your email address and a password (stored in hashed form by our authentication provider), and a display name. An account is not required to use the App.
  • Birth information (optional). If you choose to add them in Profile: your birth date, and optionally your birth time and birth place, used only to calculate your natal chart and personalize your daily note. This stays on your device unless you create an account, in which case it is synced to your private cloud profile.
  • Friends' birth details (optional). If you create a compatibility card for a friend, the birth details you enter are stored on your device to generate that card.
  • Reminders. Reminder times you set are stored on your device and delivered as local notifications. We do not operate a push-notification server.

How we use your information

  • To store and show your intentions, tasks, notes, rituals and vision board.
  • To schedule the local reminders you ask for.
  • To calculate and display your natal chart, daily note and compatibility cards, if you choose to add birth details.
  • To operate accounts, sync and security (via Supabase).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not show third-party advertising.
  • We do not send your content to any AI or language-model service. Poleris has no chat feature and makes no AI calls.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

Service providers

We use one processor to run Poleris: Supabase (authentication, database and the account-deletion function). It receives only the data needed to provide accounts and sync. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Your choices and rights

  • You can use Poleris without creating an account; your data then remains on your device.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your cloud profile and account.
  • You can request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us (see Support).

Children

Poleris is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Important note

Poleris provides content for reflection, habit-building and entertainment. It does not provide medical, psychological, legal, or financial advice.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the App.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the support page.
