Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Poleris ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Poleris mobile application (the "App").

Information we collect

  • Birth information. Your birth date, and optionally your birth time and birth place, which you provide to calculate your astrological birth chart. This stays on your device unless you create an account, in which case it is synced to your private cloud profile.
  • Account information. If you create an account: your email address and a password (stored in hashed form by our authentication provider).
  • Conversations. Messages you exchange with your AI guide are stored on your device. Recent messages are sent to our AI provider to generate responses. Messages you exchange with friends are stored on our servers so they can be delivered.
  • Content you create. Intentions, vision board photos and drawings, and compatibility cards are stored on your device.
  • Voice input (optional). If you use voice features, short audio clips are processed by our speech provider to convert them to text or to generate spoken replies. Audio is not retained by us after processing.

How we use your information

  • To calculate and display your birth chart and daily astrological content.
  • To generate personalized AI guide responses (via Anthropic's Claude API).
  • To deliver messages between you and friends you have connected with.
  • To provide optional voice features (via ElevenLabs).
  • To operate accounts, sync, and security (via Supabase).

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not show third-party advertising.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.

Service providers

We use a small number of processors to run Poleris: Supabase (authentication, database, serverless functions), Anthropic (AI responses), and ElevenLabs (optional voice features). Each receives only the data needed to provide its function.

Your choices and rights

  • You can use Poleris without creating an account; your data then remains on your device.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your cloud profile, messages, and account.
  • You can request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us (see Support).

Children

Poleris is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Important note

Poleris provides astrological content for reflection and entertainment. It does not provide medical, legal, or financial advice.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the App.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the support page.
