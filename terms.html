What Poleris is (and is not)

Poleris is a daily-practice app: intentions, the 369 method, tasks with reminders, morning and evening rituals, a 21-Day Journey, affirmations, breathing, guided meditations, sleep sounds, a vision board, streaks, a profile with stats and badges, share cards, and support messages between friends.
